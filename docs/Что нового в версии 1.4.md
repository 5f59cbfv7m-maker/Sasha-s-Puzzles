## English (U.S.)

- Play together: put the same puzzle together with family and friends, each on their own iPhone, iPad or Mac. Up to four players.
- Nearby over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth with no account, or from anywhere by inviting Game Center friends.
- See who is doing what: a piece in a partner's hand moves live with their name and colour, and their pieces light up in that colour as they snap into place.
- Everyone earns points for the pieces they put in, and the completion card shows who placed how many.
- iPhone Duo: half-open like a book, the board takes one page and the pieces the other, so nothing lies in the fold. Turned like a laptop, the board is on top and the pieces below.
- iPhone Duo as a photo frame: once the puzzle is done, stand it half-folded and the finished picture hangs in a frame; on the charger the screen stays on.
- Touch and hold the app icon to continue your last puzzle, start the daily puzzle or a quick one, or find a table nearby.
- Holidays: a new tab of eight Christmas pictures. They open on 24 December and stay for good; until then a countdown runs on every card.
- Six new tunes written for the game, three meditative and three melodic. Pick one for the library and one for the table, from the note button in the library or in Settings.
- Mix: the tunes take turns while you play, always in the style you chose.
- Medals with five levels each, bronze to diamond, for puzzles, pieces, points, daily puzzles, big puzzles, quick hands, playing together and the whole gallery.
- Play to win: the host can switch hints off for everyone at the table, and only games without hints count in Game Center challenges.
- Tables now open from the library: choose a picture and the table opens with it. In the game, tap the players over the board to invite more friends.
- Points history in Settings: what every puzzle earned and why.
- Photo credits roll like the end of a film.
- Solved pictures move to the end of their category, so the ones still to do come first.
- A “New puzzle” tag on every picture you have just earned — with points, a week of daily puzzles or a holiday — until you start it.
- Medals are Game Center achievements too, so friends can see them.
- Fixed: on narrow screens, such as iPad mini held upright, the puzzle's name in the top bar shrank to dots; the extra buttons now tuck into the ⋯ menu.
- Fixed: the welcome screen could appear again after an update for players who had already played.
- Fixed: tapping a category far down the list could leave the screen empty until you scrolled up.
- Fixed: on iPhone, the theme and table switches in Settings could break a word in two.
