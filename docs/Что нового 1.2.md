## English (U.S.)

- Your progress follows you: unfinished puzzles, your name and settings now sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac through iCloud — and come back after a reinstall.
- A new finale: the board pulls back to the whole picture, which breaks apart, puts itself back together with soft clicks, lights up along the seams and shines.
- A victory fanfare and confetti when the puzzle is done; the music softens during the finale and returns when you play on.
- Apple Pencil Pro: a gentle tap in the Pencil when a piece snaps into place, tray pieces rise slightly as the Pencil hovers over them, squeeze for a hint, double-tap to undo.
- Separate Scatter Pieces and Gather Pieces buttons.
- On iPad, a two-column tray with bigger pieces and more room for the board.
- On iPhone, bigger pieces in the tray, a tidier layout in both orientations, a larger picture at the start and stronger snapping by default.
- On Mac, the zoom buttons moved to the top bar, out of the way of the pieces.
- A cleaner game screen on iPhone and iPad: pinch to zoom.
- A special sound for three correct pieces in a row.
- Library previews show how far you got in unfinished puzzles.
- Progress bars on achievements, and achievements you've seen stop showing as new on all your devices.
- Photo credits in Settings.
- Smoother music transitions and more natural piece dragging: a piece keeps its size, casts a soft shadow and settles when you let go.
- Fixed: "Play again" could get stuck on the loading screen, and the pause and completion cards ran off the iPhone screen.
